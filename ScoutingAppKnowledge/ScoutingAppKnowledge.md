Scouting App Knowledge
======================

This section is dedicated to writing about specific portions of our scouting
app. You can read here to learn what you need to know about the app.

## Overview

This is an overview of our scouting app and how to use it. Our scouting app is
separated into two sections, `client/` and `server/`.

`client/` is the directory for the client, i.e. the actual web interface.
Everything related to the front-end is here.

`server/` is the back-end server for our app, where all of the data is
processed. It provides an interface to our database.
